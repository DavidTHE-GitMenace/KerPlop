KerPlop - Game Piece Descriptions
===============================

Game Overview
-------------
The player moves along a 21-position board, collects coins, avoids dangers,
and advances through two levels. All new game pieces and LevelSetup are in
the levelPieces package. The provided gameEngine package is unchanged.

Game Pieces (7 total)
---------------------

1. Flag (F)
   Class: Flag implements Drawable
   Movement: None
   Interaction: None; this class does not extend GamePiece.
   Description: A decorative flag drawn on the board. It has no effect on
   the player and is not added to the interactingPieces list.

2. ClockworkGuard (G)
   Class: ClockworkGuard extends GamePiece implements Moveable
   Movement: Moves one square each turn in its current direction. If the
   next square is occupied or outside the board, it reverses direction
   and tries the opposite square. It does not overwrite other pieces.
   Interaction: HIT when the player is at the guard's square or one
   square away; otherwise NONE. This is a distance interaction.

3. Wisp (W)
   Class: Wisp extends GamePiece implements Moveable
   Movement: Uses a random boolean on every turn to choose left or
   right. Moves one square if the chosen square is empty and in bounds;
   otherwise stays in place. This is the random movement algorithm.
   Interaction: Always NONE; it is a harmless moving piece.

4. Coin (C)
   Class: Coin extends GamePiece
   Movement: None
   Interaction: GET_POINT when the player lands on the same square;
   otherwise NONE. After collection, the coin is removed from the
   visible board and cannot award points again.

5. Portal (O)
   Class: Portal extends GamePiece
   Movement: None
   Interaction: ADVANCE when the player lands on the same square;
   otherwise NONE. The game advances to the next level (or wins after
   the final level).

6. SpikeTrap (T)
   Class: SpikeTrap extends GamePiece
   Movement: None
   Interaction: HIT when the player lands on the same square;
   otherwise NONE. This piece can damage the player.

7. LaserTurret (L)
   Class: LaserTurret extends GamePiece
   Movement: None
   Interaction: KILL when the player is within two positions on either
   side of the turret (including its own square); otherwise NONE. The
   laser ignores pieces between itself and the player. This is another
   distance interaction.

Levels
------
Level 1: Player starts at 10. Flag at 2, ClockworkGuard at 4,
Wisp at 7, Coin at 11, Portal at 13, SpikeTrap at 16,
LaserTurret at 20.

Level 2: Player starts at 10. ClockworkGuard at 3, Wisp at 7,
Coin at 11, Portal at 14, SpikeTrap at 16, Flag at 18,
LaserTurret at 20.

LevelSetup creates a Drawable[] board, an ArrayList<Moveable> of moving
pieces, and an ArrayList<GamePiece> of interacting pieces. Moving pieces
are added to all appropriate structures; stationary interactive pieces
are added to the board and interacting list; the Flag is added only to
the board.

Requirements Checklist
----------------------
- 7 total piece classes: Flag, ClockworkGuard, Wisp, Coin, Portal,
  SpikeTrap, LaserTurret.
- 1 Drawable-only piece: Flag.
- 2 Moveable GamePieces: ClockworkGuard and Wisp.
- 2 different movement algorithms: reversing patrol and random drift.
- Random decision: Wisp chooses its movement direction randomly.
- 4 stationary interactive GamePieces: Coin, Portal, SpikeTrap,
  LaserTurret.
- All InteractionResult values used: NONE, HIT, KILL, ADVANCE,
  GET_POINT.
- Distance interactions: ClockworkGuard (range 1) and
  LaserTurret (range 2).
- Exactly 3 pieces interact only on the same square: Coin, Portal,
  SpikeTrap.
- 2 levels defined in LevelSetup.
